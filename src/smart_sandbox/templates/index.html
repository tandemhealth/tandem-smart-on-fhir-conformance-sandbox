Test your SMART on FHIR integration against Tandem

This sandbox plays Tandem's role in the integration described in the partner guide: it accepts your EHR launch, discovers your SMART configuration, runs the PKCE authorization-code flow, reads Patient and Encounter, and writes back a sample DocumentReference and Condition resources — validating every step and showing you a detailed conformance report.

Register your EHR

How it works

  1. Register above — you get a launch URL and a private dashboard.
  2. Point your EHR's launch button at the launch URL (with iss and launch).
  3. The sandbox runs the full SMART flow against your servers and records a step-by-step report.
  4. From the run report, trigger the note + diagnoses write-back and the refresh-token test.
